Official Biography

The Vipress Story

Formed in the vibrant underground music circuit of Kolkata, Vipress has evolved into one of Eastern India's most ferocious live acts. Known for seamless genre fusion between progressive rock intensity, alternative grunge melodies, and hard-hitting Bengali anthems, the band commands stages from university amphitheatres to national music arenas.

Their studio master tracks have garnered over 1.2 million streams across platforms, while their electrifying live performances have headlined over 120 concerts. When Vipress steps onto a stage, expectations are shattered and audiences are ignited.
